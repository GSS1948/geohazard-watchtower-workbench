## Overview
The **Global Watchtower Workbench** is an open-source, institutional-grade research platform designed to solve the primary bottleneck in emergency management: **latency**. 

By federating 34 high-risk global macro-corridors and combining 5-channel multi-physics telemetry with dual-threshold autoencoder gating ($\tau_2$), this system shifts disaster mitigation permanently from post-event rescue to **72-hour proactive relocation**.

---

## Key Architectural Features
1. **Federated 34-Node Planetary Registry:** Concentrates processing power strictly on high-volatility structural bottlenecks rather than static regional baselines.
2. **Multi-Physics Signal Fusion:** Concurrent tracking of Diurnal Thermal Expansion ($\theta_{\text{thermal}}$), Hydrological Moisture Load ($M_{\text{hydro}}$), Barometric Pressure Drops ($\Delta P_{\text{baro}}$), Wind Buffeting ($V_{\text{wind}}$), and Micro-Shift Strain Acceleration ($W_{\text{strain}}$).
3. **Automated 5km Safe-Haven Matrix:** Generates $10 \times 10$ spatial grid projections upon breaching Red Alert thresholds, exporting lightweight CSV dispatch logs with direct Google Maps routing links.
